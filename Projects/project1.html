Static routing is a method where a network administrator manually configures fixed paths into a router's routing table rather than using dynamic protocols. It is often used in small networks or for specific routes that do not change frequently, providing simplicity and control over routing decisions.

ip route [destination-network] [subnet-mask] [next-hop-ip | exit-interface]
